Kalshi Sandbox Take-Home
Overview
Build a working project using the Kalshi sandbox that explores a market, trading idea, or market analysis approach of your choice. Use the Kalshi sandbox docs here: https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/demo_env

We are intentionally keeping the ask open-ended. The goal is not to produce a polished production system. We are primarily interested in how you work with real market data, how you structure a small but complete project, and how clearly you explain what you built and what you learned.

The Deliverable
Your submission should be a working project that runs against the Kalshi sandbox and demonstrates a clear idea.

At minimum, please include:

a runnable project that uses the Kalshi sandbox
a clear focus, such as a trading strategy, market analysis, execution logic, or research workflow
some form of order book analysis and/or order flow analysis relevant to your approach
a short written explanation of your approach, assumptions, and conclusions
at least one concrete output, such as a chart, notebook, metrics table, dashboard view, or simulated trade log
We care more about a complete and thoughtful deliverable than about the specific direction you choose.

Suggested Directions
You are welcome to choose any Kalshi market or set of markets. Examples of valid approaches include but are not limited to:

a trading strategy prototype
order book or liquidity analysis
order flow or market activity analysis
execution or quoting logic
a research notebook or market study
a small dashboard
Nice to have
External reference signals or pricing anchors that help contextualize the Kalshi market you chose. Depending on the market, this could include:

news or event data
polling data
sports data
macroeconomic releases
weather data
related market prices elsewhere
Scope
Please spend no more effort than what it would take you 3-5 hours to complete.

You may use any programming language, framework, or analysis environment you prefer. We expect candidates to use AI/LLM tooling fluently. Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, etc. are part of the modern engineering toolkit. The follow-up call will probe your design choices, tradeoffs, and judgment.

Reasonable simplifications are completely fine as long as they are documented.

Submission Requirements
Please submit a zip file of your project containing:

your source code
a README with setup and run instructions
a short writeup covering your approach, assumptions, and findings
any supporting output artifacts you think are helpful
Your README should make it easy for us to understand:

what you built
how to run it
what market or hypothesis you focused on
what assumptions or simplifications you made
what you would do next with more time
What We Will Evaluate
We will primarily evaluate:

clarity of thought and judgment
ability to work with APIs and real-world data
code quality and project structure
how effectively the deliverable supports the idea you chose
market intuition and prioritization
handling of uncertainty, edge cases, and tradeoffs
clarity of written communication
We are not looking for grand claims of profitability from a short take-home. We care much more about disciplined reasoning, practical engineering, and a well-explained result.
