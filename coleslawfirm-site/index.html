A practice built on relationships, not transactions.

Founded by attorney Jennifer Coles, our firm has spent more than thirty years helping Michigan families plan for the future and navigate the present with peace of mind. From Grand Rapids to Ann Arbor and points between, we believe estate planning should feel less like paperwork and more like a conversation - one where your goals, your family, and your values lead the way.

Whether you're drafting your first will, settling a loved one's estate, or planning for long-term care, we take the time to understand your story before we write a single page.
