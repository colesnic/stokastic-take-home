A practice built on relationships, not transactions.

Founded by attorney Jennifer Coles, our firm has spent more than twenty years helping West Michigan families plan for the future and navigate the present with peace of mind. We believe estate planning should feel less like paperwork and more like a conversation — one where your goals, your family, and your values lead the way.

Whether you're drafting your first will, settling a loved one's estate, or planning for long-term care, we take the time to understand your story before we write a single page.

What We Do

Practice Areas

Focused expertise in the legal matters that shape your family's future, your legacy, and your peace of mind.

E

Estate Planning

Comprehensive plans that protect your assets, honor your wishes, and provide for the people you love — tailored to your unique circumstances.

W

Wills & Trusts

From simple wills to revocable living trusts and specialized planning vehicles, we draft documents designed to work the way you intend.

P

Probate

Compassionate, efficient representation for personal representatives and families navigating the Michigan probate process.

L

Elder Law

Planning for long-term care, Medicaid eligibility, and the legal questions that accompany aging with dignity.

A

Power of Attorney & Patient Advocate

Durable powers of attorney and patient advocate designations that put trusted decision-makers in place before they're needed.

R

Real Estate

Residential transactions, deed preparation, and property transfers handled with attention to both legal precision and family dynamics.

B

Business Formation

LLCs, corporations, and start-up counsel for Michigan entrepreneurs — from formation to ongoing governance.

G

Practical advice for small and closely-held businesses, including contracts, succession planning, and day-to-day operations.
