Services available at our Battle Creek office

Our Battle Creek office offers the full range of Coles Law Firm services:

  • Revocable Living Trust — Secure your estate while retaining full control.
  • Wills & Codicils — Make sure your wishes are clearly recorded.
  • Powers of Attorney — Put trusted decision-makers in place before they're needed.
  • Elder Law — Counsel for the legal questions aging brings.
  • Medicaid & Medicare Planning — Plan ahead to protect what you've worked to build.
